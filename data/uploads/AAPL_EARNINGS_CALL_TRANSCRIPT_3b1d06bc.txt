[Sarah Sterling - CFO]
Thank you, operator. Total revenue for the third quarter rose 14% year-over-year to $4.85 billion, driven by robust recurring enterprise subscription growth and high margin cloud analytics solutions. Operating income stood at $1.45 billion, delivering an operating margin of 29.9%, up 120 basis points compared to the prior-year period. Net profit reached $1.18 billion, with diluted EPS of $1.42. 

Our cash flow from operations remained exceptional at $1.62 billion, generating free cash flow of $1.39 billion after CapEx investments into our distributed AI infrastructure. Our balance sheet remains rock-solid with $6.2 billion in cash and marketable securities and a debt-to-assets ratio of 0.24.

[John Vance - CEO]
We are witnessing accelerated demand across global commercial banking and institutional asset management. Clients are adopting our autonomous financial intelligence modules to mitigate geopolitical volatility and optimize liquidity across international corridors.
